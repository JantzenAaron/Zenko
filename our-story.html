Established 2016 • Manila

A quiet obsession with good coffee, held for a decade.

Atelier Roast began as a single drum roaster in a converted garage, run by two former architects who missed the unhurried café culture they'd known abroad. What started as weekend cupping sessions became a roastery, then a room, then a small circle of boutique cafés.

The Long Roast

How We Got Here

A decade of small, deliberate decisions rather than one big leap.

2016

The Garage Roaster

A 5kg drum roaster and a folding table — our first cupping lab, and the first Atelier Roast blend.

2018

First Boutique Café

Our first six-table room opened, designed around one rule: nothing that clamors for attention.

2021

Direct Farm Partnerships

We began sourcing directly from micromills in Benguet, Bukidnon, and Sidama — no brokers, full traceability.

2025

A Growing Circle

Multiple branches, a private tasting salon, and a franchise program built for partners who share our pace.

What We Believe

Slowness is a feature, not a delay.

Traceable, not just "single-origin"

Every lot is bought directly from the farm or micromill that produced it, at a price we're comfortable disclosing.

Small batches, tasted daily

Our roastmasters cup every batch before it reaches a menu — consistency comes from attention, not automation.

Rooms built for lingering

Every branch is designed with the same architectural calm: quiet materials, generous seating, and no rush to turn tables.
